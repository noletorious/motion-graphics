I started a new project: **[Motion](https://motion.noletorious.com)**.

The idea is simple. Take companies I know and use every day, and ask what their brand would feel like if it were designed with motion first, not added at the end.

Each company gets a few takes. I call them versions. **v1** is my first pass at the brand as it is today. Later versions go further: a redesign of one feature, a set of new ones, or something experimental.

Two companies are live right now:

- **eBay v1 · Bright Bounce.** eBay's four colours become bouncy, flat shapes. Bids land like dots dropping onto a table.
- **Google v1 · Soft Material.** Google's colours as soft, rounded shapes with calm motion, built around Search.

Each version has a logo sting, a 15-second promo and a social loop. Everything is built in code, and you can play all of it live on the site.

## How I'm making these

I direct and AI builds. Every piece starts as a storyboard of still frames. I comment on those before anything moves, then review the real animation, pin notes to exact moments, and send it back until it feels right. The taste is mine and the speed comes from the tools. I'll write more about this workflow soon, because it changed how fast I can go from idea to finished motion.
